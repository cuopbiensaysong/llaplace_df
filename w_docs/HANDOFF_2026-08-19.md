| arm | PIT-ECE | cov@80 |

At the matched size (M=25, both S=25) the mixture is **3.5× better calibrated**, and the
mixture improves monotonically with components (9.7× from M=1 to M=25).

⚠️ **Do not read the `sampled` ladder vertically.** `mixture_M<m>` always draws 25 members;
`sampled_M<m>` has exactly `m`. So sampled's ECE "worsening" with M is the estimator gaining
resolution, not calibration changing. Proof: `sampled_M2` has the best ECE (0.0132) and
catastrophic coverage (0.232 at nominal 0.80). Only `sampled_M25` is a protocol arm.

⚠️ PIT-ECE must be pooled across batches before computing, never averaged per batch — it is a
functional of the whole empirical CDF and per-batch averaging is biased low. The code stores
raw PIT under a `_pit` key that the scalar-averaging loop skips.

## 5. 🔴 A measurement I got wrong, and corrected

I twice reported that crypto and bms_air had irregular query grids. **That was an artefact.**

Time tensors are `[B, N_entity, T]` with inactive entity slots **zero-filled**. Flattening them
without the entity mask makes padding dominate wherever most slots are empty. Correct numbers,
active rows only:

| cell | gap min | max | distinct | mean gap | share = 1 | grid deviation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **us_equity h100** | 1.0 | 4.0 | 4 | **1.458** | **78.2 %** | 6.0 |
| bms_air h168 | 1.0 | 18.0 | 4 | 1.011 | 99.76 % | 20.0 |
| crypto h100 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1 | 1.000 | 100 % | 0.0 |
| noaa_uk h168 | 1.0 | 1.0 | 1 | 1.000 | 100 % | 0.0 |

* crypto is **exactly as regular as noaa_uk** and has no missingness. Retracted.
* bms_air is **99.76 % regular**; its contribution is a *variable lead gap*, not irregular spacing.
* **us_equity is the only genuinely irregular cell — and it fails Gate 0.**

**No cell is both usable and genuinely irregular.** The paper's §10 framing problem (title
promises irregular series; the evaluation cell is a fixed regular grid) is therefore **not**
solved by bms_air. Options remain: unblock us_equity, or state plainly that irregular-query
capability is demonstrated on synthetics only.

Also withdrawn: the "zero-width gap → `log(0)` NaN" concern. With padding excluded the minimum
gap on every cell is exactly 1.0.

Written up in `w_docs/finance/FINANCE_CELLS_BLOCKED.md` §5.
